About UniVoice Networks

A New York-registered communications provider selling wholesale voice, messaging and numbers to carriers and call centers.

Who we are

UniVoice Networks LLC is a communications provider organised in the State of New York. We sell wholesale voice termination, toll-free and local numbers, A2P messaging and hosted call center tools to carriers, VoIP providers, resellers and contact centers.

We are registered with the Federal Communications Commission (FRN 0037476215, Form 499 Filer ID 838144) and hold Operating Company Number 137M.

How we work

Compliance before volume
We would rather turn away traffic than carry calls that harm consumers or the networks we depend on. Every customer is verified before activation.
Specialised routes
Separating call center, conversational and standard traffic keeps each route healthy and predictable.
People who answer
Customers deal with a small team that knows their account, their traffic and their switch.
Plain commercial terms
Clear rate decks, per-second billing and CDR-based dispute handling.

Who we serve

Carriers and VoIP operators buying US and international termination, call centers that need dialer-ready and conversational US routes, messaging aggregators, and businesses that want a hosted dialer or PBX connected to dependable routes.
