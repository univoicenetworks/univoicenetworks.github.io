Cloud PBX

A hosted business phone system with extensions, IVR, queues and call recording, connected to UniVoice voice routes.

A business phone system without the hardware

Our cloud PBX replaces on-site phone equipment with a hosted platform your team can use from desk phones, softphones or a browser. Numbers, routing rules and users are managed in one place, and voice runs over UniVoice routes.

Features

Extensions and users
Desk phones, softphones and mobile apps on the same extension plan.
IVR auto attendant
Menus that route callers to the right team, with business-hours rules.
Call queues
Distribute calls across agents with ring strategies and wait music.
Call recording
Record inbound and outbound calls for quality and compliance.
Voicemail to email
Messages delivered to your inbox as audio files.
Find-me / follow-me
Ring a sequence of devices before going to voicemail.
Conferencing
Bridge internal and external callers into one conversation.
Call forwarding and transfer
Blind and attended transfers, forwarding rules per user.
CRM integration
Connect call events to your CRM on request.

Numbers and porting

Use new local numbers or toll-free numbers, or port the numbers your customers already know.
