PBX Solution

Cost effective Cloud IP PBX switchboard with convenience.

Get Started

At UniVoice Networks, we are committed to delivering customer-centric solutions that simplify even the most complex challenges faced by businesses worldwide. One of our flagship offerings is our cloud-based PBX solution, a feature-rich platform designed for high-level customization and scalability. This solution empowers businesses of all sizes to efficiently manage their communication systems without the need for additional hardware, allowing them to leverage their existing VoIP phones seamlessly.

Our cloud PBX platform provides unparalleled flexibility, enabling enterprises to configure call routing, manage extensions, and integrate advanced telephony features according to their unique operational requirements. By removing the dependency on additional equipment and centralizing communication management in the cloud, UniVoice Networks ensures that businesses can scale quickly and cost-effectively while maintaining top-tier voice quality and reliability.

To further enhance operational efficiency, we have introduced our unique real-time analytics and statistics solution, which allows managers and administrators to monitor call center productivity, track KPIs, and gain actionable insights into business performance. With this dashboard, businesses can listen to call recordings, review extension activity, and make informed decisions that drive growth and optimize workforce performance.

UniVoice Networks’ cloud PBX and real-time monitoring solutions are designed to serve enterprises of all sizes—from small businesses to large call centers—ensuring superior service, operational efficiency, and the tools needed to achieve measurable business growth. By combining advanced features, easy management, and insightful analytics, we empower businesses to optimize their communication systems, enhance agent performance, and elevate overall customer satisfaction across every interaction.
