Compliance & Acceptable Use Policy

At UniVoice Networks, we are committed to operating as a responsible and fully compliant telecommunications service provider. We adhere strictly to all applicable Federal Communications Commission (FCC) rules and regulations, as well as other relevant federal, state, and international telecom laws governing voice traffic and communication services.

Regulatory Compliance

UniVoice Networks maintains internal compliance procedures designed to ensure that our network infrastructure, routing practices, and customer operations align with current FCC requirements. We actively monitor traffic patterns, implement verification procedures, and maintain operational safeguards to prevent misuse of our services.

We fully support lawful telecommunications practices and cooperate with regulatory authorities when required.

Zero Tolerance Policy

We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against:

  • Spam or unsolicited bulk calling

  • Fraudulent traffic or scam-related activity

  • Illegal robocalling

  • Caller ID spoofing intended to mislead or deceive

  • Any traffic that violates FCC regulations or applicable laws

Any customer found engaging in prohibited activities will face immediate suspension or termination of services without prior notice.

Customer Verification & Monitoring

To maintain network integrity, all clients are subject to business verification procedures prior to activation. UniVoice Networks reserves the right to request supporting documentation and conduct due diligence reviews.

We continuously monitor network activity to detect irregular, abusive, or non-compliant traffic patterns. If suspicious activity is detected, we may take corrective action, including traffic blocking, suspension, or reporting to appropriate authorities where necessary.

Responsible Network Usage

Our services are intended strictly for lawful, professional business use. Customers are responsible for ensuring their operations comply with all applicable regulations, including consumer protection and telemarketing laws.

UniVoice Networks is committed to maintaining a secure, high-quality network environment for legitimate call centers, telecom operators, and business clients.
