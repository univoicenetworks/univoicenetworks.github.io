Local numbers, delivered over SIP

Direct inward dialing (DID) numbers give your customers a local number to call. We provide US local numbers across many rate centers and deliver inbound calls to your SIP endpoint, ready for your PBX, contact center or resale platform.

What is included

Wide selection
Local numbers across US area codes and rate centers, subject to availability.
Number porting
Port existing numbers in with a letter of authorisation.
Inbound delivery
Calls routed to your SIP endpoint, with failover options.
Fair use of numbers
Numbers used for outbound caller ID must stay assigned to the customer using them.

Number use and caller ID

Numbers we assign may be used as outbound caller ID only by the customer they are assigned to. We do not lease numbers for rotating or “neighbour spoofing” caller ID schemes, and we reclaim numbers that are used that way.
