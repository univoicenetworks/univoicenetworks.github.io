DID Services

Powerpack inbound calling services with nation-wide footprint

Get Started

UniVoice Networks provides SIP-based handoff solutions designed to support stable, high-capacity inbound calling for carriers and call centers. Through our Direct Inward Dialing (DID) origination services, clients can instantly access multiple numbers across various U.S. markets for immediate deployment. Our robust origination infrastructure ensures reliable connectivity, consistent call quality, and scalable inbound traffic management.

We offer customized service packages tailored to individual telephone numbers (TNs), delivering maximum flexibility and control. Our DID solutions include advanced features such as CNAM caller ID storage, number management, and configurable routing options to meet diverse business requirements.

With coverage across multiple U.S. rate centers, UniVoice Networks is rapidly becoming a preferred provider for inbound origination services. We also support number portability, allowing customers to transfer existing local numbers to UniVoice Networks, ensuring seamless continuity and cost efficiency without service disruption.
