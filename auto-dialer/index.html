Auto dialer

A hosted predictive dialer for compliant outbound call centers, with recording, scripting, DNC tools and live monitoring.

More conversations per agent hour

Our hosted dialer keeps agents talking instead of waiting. Predictive and progressive modes pace calls to your team’s availability, and calls connect to agents only when a person answers. Because the dialer and the carrier routes come from one provider, troubleshooting takes one phone call.

Features

Dialing modes
Predictive, progressive, preview and manual, configurable per campaign.
Call recording
Record calls for quality assurance, training and dispute resolution.
Agent scripting
Dynamic scripts that guide agents through each call.
DNC management
Internal DNC lists and scrubbing against lists you upload.
Time-zone controls
Calling windows based on the called party’s local time.
Inbound and blended
Take inbound calls and callbacks on the same agent seats.
Live monitoring
Listen, whisper and barge for coaching and supervision.
Real-time dashboards
Campaign, list and agent metrics as they happen.
Webphone
Browser-based softphone, so agents need only a headset.

Compliance tools, not shortcuts

The dialer gives you the controls compliant calling needs: abandoned-call limits, calling-hour windows, DNC lists and consent fields. Using them correctly remains your responsibility under the TCPA and state law, and dialer traffic is subject to our acceptable use policy.
