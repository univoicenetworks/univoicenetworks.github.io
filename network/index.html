Network and interconnect

How to connect to UniVoice Networks: SIP signalling, authentication, codecs and the operational practices behind our routes.

Interconnect specifications

ProtocolSIP (RFC 3261) over UDP or TCP
AuthenticationSource IP address; digest authentication available for selected products
Number formatE.164; US numbers as 1 + 10 digits
CodecsG.711 µ-law, G.711 A-law, G.729
DTMFRFC 2833 / RFC 4733
CapacityConcurrent-call and calls-per-second limits set per account
Caller IDPassed as sent when the customer is authorised to use the number

How we run the network

Traffic monitoring
ASR, ACD, short-duration ratio and post-dial delay are tracked per customer and per route.
Anomaly response
Unusual patterns — sudden volume spikes, very short calls, invalid caller ID — are investigated and can be suspended while we review them.
Fraud controls
Spending limits, destination restrictions and alerts protect customers from compromised systems.
Change control
Route and rate changes are communicated in advance, as your agreement sets out.

Getting connected

Once your account passes onboarding, we exchange technical details: your signalling IP addresses, our gateway addresses, any technical prefix and your capacity limits. Test calls are completed before live traffic starts.
