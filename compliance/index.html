Our compliance program

Know your customer
We verify every customer’s legal identity, business address, ownership, intended traffic and, for US providers, their FCC registrations before enabling service.
Contract terms
Customers agree in writing to carry only lawful traffic, to use caller ID they are authorised to use, and to cooperate with traceback.
Traffic analytics
Call patterns are monitored for signs of illegal robocalling, spoofing, traffic pumping and fraud.
Traceback cooperation
We respond to Industry Traceback Group and law enforcement traceback requests fully and within 24 hours.
Enforcement
Traffic that violates our policies is blocked, and accounts responsible for it are suspended or terminated.
