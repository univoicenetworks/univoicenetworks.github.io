Carrier-to-carrier voice

UniVoice Networks sells wholesale voice to licensed and registered service providers: carriers, VoIP operators, resellers and enterprises with their own switching. We interconnect over SIP, bill per second, and keep the commercial relationship straightforward.

Route options

USA call center
Dialer-tolerant routes for lawful outbound campaigns. Details
USA conversational
High-ASR routes for live, long-duration calls. Details
USA and Canada standard
Wholesale termination for retail and business calling.
International A–Z
Termination to international destinations, with route quality options by destination.
Toll-free termination
Calls to US and Canadian 8YY numbers. Details

How we work with carriers

Rate decks
Rate decks by email in standard formats, with notice of changes in line with your agreement.
Testing
Test calls and a trial balance before you commit volume.
Billing
Prepaid by default. Postpaid terms can be discussed after a trading history is established.
Disputes
CDR-level reconciliation, handled by people who can read a CDR.
Two-way trading
If you also sell routes, we are open to buying from carriers that meet our compliance requirements.

Compliance comes first

Every account goes through know-your-customer review before traffic is enabled, and all traffic is subject to our acceptable use policy and robocall mitigation program.
