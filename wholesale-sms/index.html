Wholesale SMS

A2P messaging routes for aggregators, platforms and enterprises sending legitimate, opt-in traffic.

Request interconnectAsk for a rate deck

Messaging routes for opt-in traffic

We provide wholesale SMS routes to messaging aggregators, software platforms and enterprises that send transactional and marketing messages to people who have opted in. Connect by SMPP or HTTP API and receive delivery reports for every message.

What you get

Connectivity
SMPP 3.4 binds or HTTP API, with test credentials during onboarding.
Delivery reports
DLRs returned for every submitted message.
Use cases
One-time passwords, alerts, appointment reminders, order updates and permission-based marketing.
Unicode
Support for non-Latin character sets and concatenated long messages.

US messaging requirements

US carriers require business messaging to be registered. Messages sent from 10-digit long codes need an approved 10DLC brand and campaign through The Campaign Registry, and toll-free senders need toll-free verification. We help customers understand these requirements and do not accept unregistered US traffic.

Content we do not carry

Messages without the recipient’s consent, phishing or impersonation, and content prohibited by carrier codes of conduct, including unlawful sex, hate, alcohol, firearms and tobacco (“SHAFT”) content where restricted. Every message must honour STOP and other opt-out keywords.
