Toll-free origination

US and Canadian toll-free numbers that ring into your switch, PBX or contact center over SIP.

Request interconnectAsk for a rate deck

Inbound toll-free, delivered over SIP

Toll-free numbers let customers reach you at no cost to them. We provision 800, 833, 844, 855, 866, 877 and 888 numbers and deliver calls to your SIP endpoint, so you can route them through your own IVR, PBX or contact center platform.

What is included

Number provisioning
New toll-free numbers, subject to availability through the toll-free number administrator.
Porting
Bring your existing toll-free numbers with a letter of authorisation and a recent invoice.
SIP delivery
Calls delivered to one or more endpoints, with failover on request.
Coverage
Callers from the US and Canada. Origination from payphones and some mobile networks may vary.
Reporting
Inbound CDRs for usage reconciliation and call analytics.

Good fits

Customer support lines, sales hotlines, marketing campaign numbers that track response by channel, and service providers that resell toll-free numbers to their own customers.
