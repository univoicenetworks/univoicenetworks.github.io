Toll-free/8YY Origination

Connectivity made easier with toll-free/8YY origination.

Get Started

UniVoice Networks offers seamless toll-free number portability, allowing you to transfer your existing Toll-Free (8YY) numbers to our network without service interruption. We also provide a wide inventory of toll-free numbers across the United States and Canada, enabling businesses to expand their national presence with fast provisioning and reliable activation. Our origination platform efficiently routes inbound traffic from VoIP networks, wireless carriers, cable providers, and traditional PSTN lines through major inter-exchange carriers to ensure stable connectivity and premium voice quality.

With extensive network coverage and direct connectivity to leading LEC tandems and underlying carriers, UniVoice Networks operates as a dependable, one-stop provider for nationwide Toll-Free (8YY) origination. Our scalable, enterprise-grade infrastructure is designed to support call centers, enterprises, and telecom operators with consistent performance, optimized routing, and seamless communication across North America.
