USA conversational routes

High-quality US termination for live, agent-to-customer conversations, where clear audio and stable caller ID matter more than call volume.

Request interconnectAsk for a rate deck

Built for calls that last

Conversational traffic is measured in minutes, not seconds. Customer service teams, account managers, telehealth and support desks need audio that stays clear for the whole call and caller ID that arrives intact. Our conversational routes are reserved for this kind of traffic, which keeps them clean and keeps quality high for everyone using them.

What you get

Voice quality
Routes selected for audio clarity, low post-dial delay and stable connections on long calls.
CLI delivery
Caller ID is passed through as sent, provided it is a number you are authorised to use.
DTMF
RFC 2833 / RFC 4733 DTMF for IVRs and payment lines.
Quality monitoring
ASR and ACD are tracked per account. If quality drops, we investigate the route rather than ask you to prove it.
Flexible capacity
Concurrent-call limits that match your agent count, adjusted as your team grows.

Who uses conversational routes

Contact centers handling inbound callbacks and customer care, sales teams calling existing customers, VoIP providers serving business end users, and resellers who need a dependable US route for retail traffic.

Dialer campaigns with short average call duration belong on our call center routes. Keeping the two traffic types apart is how both stay healthy.

Compliance comes first

Every account goes through know-your-customer review before traffic is enabled, and all traffic is subject to our acceptable use policy and robocall mitigation program.
