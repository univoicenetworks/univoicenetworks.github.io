Toll-free termination

Wholesale termination for calls placed to US and Canadian toll-free numbers, routed to the responsible carrier.

Request interconnectAsk for a rate deck

Reach every toll-free number

When your customers dial an 800, 833, 844, 855, 866, 877 or 888 number, the call needs to reach the carrier that serves it. Our toll-free termination routes do that for wholesale customers who would rather not maintain those interconnects themselves.

Why buy toll-free termination from us

One interconnect
Toll-free termination on the same SIP trunk as your other UniVoice routes.
Clean traffic only
Toll-free traffic pumping and artificially inflated traffic are prohibited and monitored for.
Clear pricing
Per-minute rates in a single deck, with no surprise surcharges.
Support
Help from people who understand 8YY routing and database dips.

Compliance comes first

Every account goes through know-your-customer review before traffic is enabled, and all traffic is subject to our acceptable use policy and robocall mitigation program.
